## 🖼️ Art Genre  / Prompts

| Genre                         | Advanced Art Style Prompt |
|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Gothic Psych-Noir**         | A dark, gritty graphic novel aesthetic featuring heavy ink-wash textures and obsessive cross-hatching. High-contrast chiaroscuro lighting with a palette of midnight indigo, deep violet, and sickly teals. Faces are expressive and weathered; shadows are deep and filled with fine-line detail. Atmospheric noir cityscapes with glowing windows and misty, stippled gradients. |
| **Esoteric Maximalism**       | Intricate, multi-layered comic book composition with ornate, mystical paneling. Incorporates sacred geometry, circular vignettes, and symmetrical occult symbols. The style blends sharp, fine-point pen ink work with rich, flat colors. Features surreal elements like third eyes, cosmic energy swirls, and Art Nouveau-inspired decorative borders. |
| **Underground 'Zine' Grunge** | Low-brow, counter-culture comic art style with a heavy newsprint texture. Features visible halftone dot patterns, intentional ink bleeds, and dirty paper grain. The color palette is limited and muted, evoking 1970s underground press. Character designs are edgy and slightly distorted, with messy, aggressive brushwork and splattered ink details. |
| **Eldritch Surrealism**       | Visceral body-horror meets high-concept fantasy. Features organic, tentacled forms, melting anatomical details, and fluid, liquid-like silhouettes. The linework is sharp and precise, contrasting with chaotic, psychedelic color washes. Backgrounds are surreal and dreamlike, utilizing double-exposure effects and shifting perspectives. |
| **Post-Punk Graphic Expressionism** | A raw, frantic artistic style characterized by sharp, angular lines and high-energy charcoal-pencil textures. Deep blacks dominate the frame, accented by sharp pops of primary colors. Characters look gaunt and dramatic, positioned in cinematic, low-angle shots. The overall feel is sketchbook meets high-fashion horror, focusing on mood over realism. |
| **Occult Pop-Art**            | A blend of vintage occult illustration and 1960s pop-art. Features bold, thick ink outlines with flat, saturated color fields. Use of stippling for shading and textured overlays that mimic old lithographs. High-contrast imagery involving skeletons, tarot-like figures, and esoteric runes, rendered with a clean but sinister *Ligne Claire* influence. |
| **Abstract Ink-Wash Horror**  | Gothic horror rendered in a mix of traditional ink and digital watercolor. Features bleeding edges, erratic ink splatters, and ghostly, desaturated color palettes. Figures often appear as silhouettes or are partially obscured by atmospheric smoke and scratch-board textures. Minimalist backgrounds with a focus on emotional, haunting character expressions. |
