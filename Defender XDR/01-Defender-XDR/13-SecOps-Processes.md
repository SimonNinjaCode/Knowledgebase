## Process Template

All processes follow a standard template structure:

1. **Purpose** - Why this process exists
2. **Scope** - What the process covers
3. **Roles and Responsibilities** - Who does what
4. **Process Steps** - Detailed procedures
5. **Escalation** - When and how to escalate
6. **Related Documentation** - Links to other docs
7. **Revision History** - Document changes
