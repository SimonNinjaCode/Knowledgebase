# Introduction to Viva Insights

## Översikt

Viva Insights — en del av Microsoft 365 E7. Se [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/viva/insights/introduction) för full dokumentation.

## Innehåll

Microsoft Viva Insights is part of [Microsoft Viva]. Viva Insights helps people and businesses thrive with data-driven, privacy-protected insights and offers recommendations to improve productivity and wellbeing. It provides personal insights to your employees, team insights for managers, organization insights for your managers and business leaders, and advanced analysis tools for analysts to address critical questions about work culture.

![Screenshot that shows the landing page for the Copilot Dashboard.]

Viva Insights has two different sets of features and experiences: the Viva Insights web app and Viva Insights in Teams. Let\'s dive in to each.

## Viva Insights web app

The standalone Viva Insights web app provides more robust analysis tools and custom reports to provide deeper insights about employee collaboration, as well as usage and adoption of Copilot.

- **Microsoft Copilot Dashboard** helps organizations maximize the value of Microsoft 365 Copilot. It provides actionable insights to help your organization get ready to deploy AI, drive adoption based on how AI is transforming workplace behavior, and measure the impact of Copilot. The dashboard covers the following categories of metrics: Readiness, adoption, impact, and sentiment. [Learn more about the Copilot Dashboard].

- **[Copilot Analytics reports]** provide a variety of targeted insights about the adoption and impact of Copilot and AI agents across your organization, as well as other types of insights related to broader employee collaboration patterns. These reports include out-of-the-box reports for leaders as well as deep dive reports that can be accessed and customized by analysts and shared with business leaders.

- **[Advanced analysis tools]** help analysts address critical questions about AI transformation, organizational resiliency and work culture, with insight into how work and AI impacts their people and their business. Leaders can see opportunities where a change in process or technology could improve business outcomes and take steps to protect employee wellbeing.

The different tools in Viva Insights are available in varying levels to users of different Microsoft 365 and Office 365 plans. [Learn more about which features are included in which plans].

### Feature availability in Government Community Cloud (GCC) environments

In GCC environments, the Viva Insights web app has many but not all of the same features as in Worldwide Multi-tenant environments. [Learn more about which Viva Insights features are available in GCC environments].

## Viva Insights in Teams

The Viva Insights app in Teams includes insights for your employees:

- **[Personal insights]** supply private, personalized feedback for individual employees. These insights help people learn about their work patterns, how to improve their wellbeing, and boost productivity. Example recommendations are protecting time in the day for regular breaks and scheduling time for focused work and learning.

- **[Team insights]** provides much-needed visibility into work habits that might lead to burnout and stress. These habits include regular after-hours work, meeting overload, or too little focus time. People can use these insights to help their teams strike a balance between productivity and wellbeing. They can also learn how their habits impact their team while getting suggestions on how to foster team culture.

  ::: NOTE

  Organizational behavior insights reports in the Viva Insights Teams app are no longer available. To access these types of insights, analysts can publish collaboration insights through the [Publish reports feature]. With this change, collaboration metrics and insights -- in addition to Copilot insights -- are all available together in the Viva Insights web app. [Learn more].
  :::
