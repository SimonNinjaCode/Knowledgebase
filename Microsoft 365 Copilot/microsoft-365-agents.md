# Agents in Microsoft 365 &#8211; Microsoft Adoption

## Översikt

Microsoft 365 Agents — en del av Microsoft 365 E7. Se [Microsoft Learn](https://adoption.microsoft.com/ai-agents/agents-in-microsoft-365/) för full dokumentation.

## Innehåll

Meet the new agents in Microsoft 365---purpose-built to bring specialized skills and knowledge into Microsoft 365 Copilot. Embedded directly in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat and the Microsoft 365 apps you use every day, these out-of-the-box agents take on unique roles to work alongside you and your team. This collaboration makes your work easier, keeps teams aligned, and helps accelerate decision-making to unlock faster business value.

## Deliver strategic insights and research

The Researcher agent works within Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat to synthesize information, brainstorm ideas, and create quality in-depth content quickly and easily, enabling you and your team to tackle complex research tasks efficiently.\
*Generally available.*

[Announcement blog][][Learn more]

## Unlock advanced data analysis

The Analyst agent works within Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat to transform complex data into clear insights and visualizations, enabling you and your team to make informed decisions quickly and efficiently.\
*Generally available.*

[Announcement blog][]Learn more

## Manage meetings more effectively

The Facilitator agent works alongside you and your team to help you manage Teams meetings effortlessly by taking notes, getting quick answers, and moderating the conversation.\
*Generally available.*

[Technical documentation][][User help & learning]

## Break down language barriers

The Interpreter agent enables real-time speech-to-speech interpretation in Teams meetings so each participant can speak and listen in the language of their choice in up to nine languages.

*Generally available.*

## Streamline project management

The Project Manager agent automates project management in Planner, handling everything from plan creation, generating status reports, and executing tasks.\
