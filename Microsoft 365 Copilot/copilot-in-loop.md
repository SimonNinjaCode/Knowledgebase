# Frequently asked questions about Copilot in Loop

*Last updated: November 3, 2025*

## What can Copilot in Loop do?

Copilot combines the power of large language models (LLMs) with Loop to help you more easily ask questions, create content, and collaborate with your peers.

## Why did my Copilot experience change in late 2025?

We recently removed some Copilot integrations from the Loop app in preparation to synchronize the experience with Microsoft 365 Copilot Pages experience. The differences were leading to user confusion. 

## What\'s the plan for bringing those capabilities back?

We\'re actively working to bring some Copilot experiences from Microsoft 365 Copilot Pages into Loop and upgrade the Copilot chat to match the same model used in the Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat + Microsoft 365 Copilot Pages experience. This will ensure responses and interactions are consistent across products. In the meantime, you can still access the reduced functionality Copilot chat integration in Loop by selecting the Copilot button in the upper-right corner to refine content. 

## What Microsoft 365 plans & licenses do I need to use Copilot in Loop? 

A Microsoft 365 Copilot license is required to use Copilot in Loop. In addition, a Microsoft 365 E3 or E5 license is required to purchase a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. [Learn more about purchasing Microsoft 365 Copilot.](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/enterprise/microsoft-365-copilot#Pricing)

People with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license can use Copilot in Loop and share with others within their organizations. People in their organizations without a Copilot license have a limited ability to view and edit content created with Copilot.
