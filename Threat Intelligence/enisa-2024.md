# ENISA Threat Landscape 2024
## Cybersecurity Threat Statistics and Trends July 2023 - June 2024

### Prime Cyber Security Threats
- Ransomware
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS)
- Malware
- Social Engineering
- Supply Chain Attacks
- Threats against data
- Information manipulation and interference

### Attack Trends & Statistics

- **Most Common Attacks:** Ransomware and DDoS attacks represent over half of reported incidents.

- **Ransomware Attacks:** Stabilised at high numbers compared to previous period.
  Top strains: LockBit, Cl0p, and PLAY (used in RaaS and extortion).

- **Malware and Information Stealers:** Significant increase in incidents, particularly information stealers. AiTM and identity based attacks on the rise. Attributed to state-linked actors and XaaS growth. Common examples: RedLine, Vidar, Raccoon Stealer, Snake Keylogger.

- **Phishing:** Phishing and pretexting via email remain a leading cause of incidents.

- **Data Breaches:** Significant threat, with attackers combining advanced techniques (e.g., phishing and ransomware). 78% increase in reported data compromises compared to 2022. Average total cost of a data breach increased by 2.3% from 2022.

- **DDoS Attacks:** Increase in scale and complexity.

- **Information Manipulation:** Increase in incidents, particularly around major events (e.g., elections).
