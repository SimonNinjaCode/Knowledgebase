# 🇸🇪 Cybersäkerhet i Sverige 2024

Cybersecurity is a shared responsibility requiring both governmental and organizational efforts.  
Proactive, systematic approaches are the recommended way of working to mitigate emerging threats.

---

## 🧨 Key Threats and Actors

**State-Sponsored Threats**  
Target national security, critical infrastructure, and businesses through cyber espionage and sabotage to gain geopolitical and economic advantages.

**Cybercriminals**  
Motivated by financial gain, using ransomware, phishing, and fraud to exploit victims.

**Ideologically Driven Actors**  
Hacktivists and similar groups conduct attacks for ideological purposes, such as DDoS or website defacement.

---

## 🔓 Common Attack Methods

- **Phishing and Spear-Phishing**: Exploit human vulnerabilities to steal sensitive information or install malware.
- **Supply Chain Attacks**: Compromise third-party vendors to infiltrate targets.
- **Exploitation of Technical Weaknesses**: Outdated software, weak passwords, and poor system configurations.

---

## ⚠️ Current Weaknesses

- **Lack of Structured Security Measures**  
  Many organizations lack systematic cybersecurity practices, increasing their exposure.

- **Competence Shortage**  
  A significant gap in cybersecurity expertise exists, requiring investment in training and development.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- **Incident Detection and Response**  
  Establish systems to detect and respond to security incidents promptly.

- **Timely Security Updates**  
  Apply patches regularly to mitigate known vulnerabilities.

- **Strong Authentication Practices**  
  Enforce multi-factor authentication and limit high-privilege account access.

- **System Hardening**  
  Deactivate unused services/protocols and enforce robust configurations.

- **Backup and Recovery**  
  Regularly test backup systems for rapid recovery in emergencies.

- **Network Segmentation**  
  Limit malware spread and control access through proper segmentation.

- **Whitelist Software**  
  Allow only approved applications to reduce malware risk.

- **Upgrade Legacy Systems**  
  Replace outdated hardware/software with supported, secure versions.

- **Control Internet Access**  
  Use firewalls and proxies to manage and monitor all traffic.

- **Education and Training**  
  Promote awareness and preparedness across all organizational levels.
