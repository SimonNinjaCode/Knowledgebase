# Secure Future Initiative

## Introduction

Microsoft has launched the **Secure Future Initiative** as part of its long-term commitment to strengthen cybersecurity across its ecosystem. This initiative is built on three core principles:

- **Secure by Design**
- **Secure by Default**
- **Secure Operations**

The initiative is supported by a culture of continuous improvement, paved security paths, and enforced standards.

---

## Cultural Shift

Security is now a **top priority at Microsoft**, reflected in:

- Performance reviews
- Leadership compensation
- Enterprise-wide training via the **Security Skilling Academy**

---

## Robust Governance

- A **Cybersecurity Governance Council** and **Deputy CISOs** have been established.
- Their mission: deliver comprehensive oversight and risk management across all areas of security.

---

## Engineering Advancements 🔧

Progress has been made across **six core engineering pillars**:

1. **Protecting identities and secrets**  
   - Hardware-backed key protection  
   - Automated key rotation  
   - Currently, **73% of Entra ID tokens** to Microsoft applications are secured by Azure’s Hardware Security Module (HSM) service

2. **Isolating production systems and tenants**  
   - Inactive elements eliminated  
   - 730,000 unused applications deleted  
   - 5.75 million inactive tenants removed  
   - 15,000 locked-down devices deployed in the last 3 months

3. **Protecting networks**  
   - Centralized asset inventory and micro-segmentation  
   - **99%** of physical assets in production tracked with ownership and firmware compliance

4. **Securing engineering systems**  
   - Centralized build pipeline templates  
   - Reduced credential lifespan  
   - **85%** of production pipelines use governed templates

5. **Enhancing threat monitoring and detection**  
   - Standardized logging libraries across services  
   - Log retention extended to **2 years**  
   - Advanced analytics applied for threat insights

6. **Accelerating response and remediation**  
   - Reduced time-to-mitigate  
   - Increased response transparency
