# Cybersecurity in the Union 2024

ENISA – State of Cybersecurity in the Union Report 2024
📄 [Full Report Link](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/2024-report-on-the-state-of-the-cybersecurity-in-the-union)

An evidence-based overview of the cybersecurity landscape and capabilities across the European Union (EU).

---

## 🔐 Cybersecurity Threats – Summary

### Ransomware Evolves

- Focus is shifting from encryption to **data exfiltration**.
- SMEs are increasingly targeted.
- **Double extortion** (encryption + data leak threats) is becoming standard.

### Social Engineering & AI

- Criminals are leveraging:
  - Phishing emails
  - Social media
  - AI-generated content (e.g., deepfakes)
- Objective: steal credentials and impersonate identities.

### Hacker-for-Hire Services

- These services are **professionalizing cybercrime**, offering capabilities to:
  - Private entities
  - State-sponsored actors

### Law Enforcement Impact

- Arrests and takedowns are:
  - Disrupting criminal infrastructure
  - Forcing threat actors to adapt or pivot to new business models

## Societal Cyber Resilience

### Union-wide Cybersecurity Maturity & Criticality

- **Many EU citizens lack the digital skills** required to fully participate in society.
- This limits access to essential online services and weakens overall resilience.
- **Key insights:**
  - Only **35% of EU citizens aged 55–74** have basic digital skills.
  - Just **29% of retired/inactive citizens** meet the same baseline.
  - In contrast, **over 70%** of young adults or individuals with higher education do.
