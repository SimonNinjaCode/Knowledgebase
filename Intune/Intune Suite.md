# Microsoft Intune advanced capabilities and licensing

Do not keep fixed per-user prices in this knowledge base. Pricing, bundles, and
regional availability change independently of the technical capabilities. Use
the official pricing page for a current quote.

Starting in July 2026, Microsoft distributes advanced Intune capabilities across
Microsoft 365 license tiers:

| License | Included Intune capabilities |
|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 E3 | Intune Plan 2, Remote Help, and Advanced Analytics |
| Microsoft 365 E5 and E7 | E3 capabilities plus Endpoint Privilege Management, Microsoft Cloud PKI, and Enterprise Application Management |
| Other eligible plans | Intune Suite or individual capabilities can be licensed separately, subject to the current product terms |

Intune Plan 2 and the Intune Suite can also include capabilities such as
Microsoft Tunnel for MAM, specialty-device management, and firmware over-the-air
updates. Confirm the exact entitlement for the tenant before designing or
enabling a feature.
