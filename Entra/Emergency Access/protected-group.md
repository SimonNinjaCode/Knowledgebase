# 🛠️ Break Glass Protection

**Role-assignable groups** benefit from extra protections compared to non-role-assignable groups:

- Only the **Global Administrator**, **Privileged Role Administrator**, or the **group Owner** can manage the group.
- No other users can change the credentials of active group members.
- This helps prevent an admin from **self-elevating** into a higher privileged role without going through an approval workflow.

---

### 🔒 Immutable Setting

- This setting **must be configured when the group is created** and cannot be changed later.

---

**Microsoft Entra Roles can be assigned to the group:** ✅ **Yes**
